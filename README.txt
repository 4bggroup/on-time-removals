TIDEWAY REMOVALS — COASTAL HTML WEBSITE
Version 1.0 | 17 November 2026

OPEN THE WEBSITE
Extract the ZIP, open the Tideway_Removals_HTML_Website folder and double-click
index.html. Keep the assets folder beside it. No installation or build is needed.
The site also works on ordinary static web hosting.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- A new one-page white and cobalt-blue design with layered shadows.
- 18 distinct approved AI-generated service photographs.
- Responsive WebP image variants, local fonts and an SVG favicon.
- House, apartment, office and small moves; full and fragile packing; unpacking;
  furniture protection, assembly, appliances, pianos, storage and longer journeys.
- Downsizing, access advice, a saved checklist, a quote guide and 12 FAQs.
- Quick-plan prefill, service selection, suburb selection and a moving brief.
- Keyboard-accessible navigation, quote tabs, native dropdowns and dialog.
- Reduced-motion support and a print stylesheet for the checklist.

IMPORTANT FORM BEHAVIOUR
This is a self-contained design concept. The form creates a brief for review
and downloads it as a text file. It does NOT email, submit an enquiry, charge
money or reserve a date. This behaviour is explained next to the form and in
the FAQs. A live form endpoint/email or CRM integration must be connected
before accepting enquiries on a production website.
Form data stays in the open browser tab and is not saved by the website.
Only checklist ticks may be stored in this browser's local storage; Reset
clears them. There are no analytics, external fonts or third-party scripts.

BUSINESS DETAILS BEFORE LAUNCH
Tideway Removals is a sample brand and Thirroul NSW is the concept location.
Replace the name/location with the client's approved details if required.
No phone number, business address, reviews, ratings, prices or insurance
credentials have been invented. Confirm actual service coverage, business
identity, contacts, protection arrangements and booking terms before launch.
The illustrations do not depict a real removal business or its actual staff.
